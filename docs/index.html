What the numbers mean

Embark refreshes its public top-10,000 ranked leaderboard about every 30 minutes, and a script checks it every 10 minutes and compares it with the previous refresh. A changed score means that player just finished a ranked game, and Ruby means ranks 1 to 500. So the numbers trail reality by roughly 15–45 minutes, and remember the leaderboard is global while matchmaking is regional, so the heatmap mixes every region together.
